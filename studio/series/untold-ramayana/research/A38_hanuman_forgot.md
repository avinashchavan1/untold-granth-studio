## Popular version (verbatim, ramcharit.in/kishkindha-kand, after doha 29)
- "कहइ रीछपति सुनु हनुमाना। का चुप साधि रहेहु बलवाना।।"
- "राम काज लगि तब अवतारा। सुनतहिं भयउ पर्बताकारा।।" (the page has तब)
- Tulsidas gives no curse or reason for the forgetting.

## Avoid
- "until someone reminds him" as Valmiki's words (it isn't in 7.36.33).
- Mocking the popular version; both are told with devotion.
- Uttara is a later layer of the epic: say "in the Uttara Kanda, Agastya tells Rama".
