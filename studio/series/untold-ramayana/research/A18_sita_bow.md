# A18: No swayamvara crowd at the bow (research notes, Nov 21 2026)
For Vivah Panchami (Mon 14 Dec 2026). Sources: CE Bala Kanda at enjoylearningsanskrit.com book-1 ch 65, 66, 67, 70, 72, 73-75 (read);
Ramcharitmanas Bal Kand at ramcharit.in (episodes 49, 51, 52; read via fetch, verbatim text).

## Valmiki (CE), all read
- 1.65.5-6: Vishvamitra: the two princes "wish to SEE that bow"; they will go home once they've seen it. (A visit, not a contest.)
- 1.65.8-13: the bow is Shiva's (Daksha's sacrifice), left as a deposit (nyāsa) with Devarata of Nimi's line.
- 1.65.14: Sita rose from the earth as Janaka ploughed.
- 1.65.15 वीर्यशुल्केति मे कन्या स्थापितेयमयोनिजा — her bride-price is valour (vīryaśulkā).
- 1.65.16-20: kings came (EARLIER) to seek her; the bow was brought; "न शेकुर्ग्रहणे तस्य धनुषस्तोलने ऽपि वा" (19): couldn't even grasp or lift it.
- 1.65.21-25: angry, they BESIEGED Mithila for a full year; provisions ran out; the gods gave Janaka an army; the kings fled.
- 1.65.27: "if Rama strings it, I will give Sita."
- 1.66.2-4: the bow brought from the city in an eight-wheeled chest, dragged "somehow" (कथं चन). ⚠ 66.4 CE reads नृपां ("kings"?) where the vulgate has नृणां (men): DON'T state who/how many pulled it.
- 1.66.12-14: Vishvamitra: "Rama, look at the bow." Rama: "I'll touch it; I'll try to lift or draw it."
- 1.66.15 लीलया स धनुर्मध्ये जग्राह — took it by the middle, playfully; 66.16 पश्यतां नृसहस्राणां — thousands of MEN (people) watching; strung it "as if in play".
- 1.66.17 तद्बभञ्ज धनुर्मध्ये — broke it in the middle. 66.18 thunder-crash, earthquake. 66.19 all fell senseless except the sage, the king and the two Raghavas.
- 1.66.20-23: Janaka: Rama's prowess seen; my vow of vīryaśulka is true; Sita to be given. 66.24-27: messengers to Ayodhya.
- Sita is not mentioned as present in 1.65-66; no garland except "the bow adorned with fragrances and garlands" (66.2). No jaymala anywhere in 1.65-72 (grep of the translations).
- 1.72.17 (WEDDING; Vivah Panchami śloka): इयं सीता मम सुता सहधर्मचरी तव । प्रतीच्छ चैनां भद्रं ते पाणिं गृह्णीष्व पाणिना ॥
  "This is Sita, my daughter, your companion in dharma. Accept her; bless you. Take her hand in yours." 72.18: Urmila to Lakshmana.
- 1.73.1-16: AFTER the wedding, Vishvamitra leaves, Dasharatha sets out for Ayodhya; omens on the road; then Parashurama appears (73.16-22).
  1.74-75: he challenges Rama with Vishnu's bow (Jamadagni's); Rama strings it (75.4-5). So: Parashurama is on the ROAD HOME, not at the bow.
- Extra (maybe not for this reel): 1.70.17 King Sudhanvan demanded the bow and Sita, besieged Mithila, was killed by Janaka.

## Ramcharitmanas (Bal Kand), the version on TV
- Before doha 250: "सोइ पुरारि कोदंडु कठोरा। राज समाज आजु जोइ तोरा॥" (whoever in this royal assembly breaks it today...)
- Before doha 251: "भूप सहस दस एकहि बारा। लगे उठावन टरइ न टारा॥" (ten thousand kings at once couldn't move it)
- Before doha 264: "सियँ जयमाल राम उर मेली॥" (Sita placed the victory garland on Rama)
- Before doha 268: "तेहिं अवसर सुनि सिवधनु भंगा। आयउ भृगुकुल कमल पतंगा॥" (Parashurama arrives at that moment, hearing the bow broke)

## Angle (respectful)
- Popular: a swayamvara assembly of kings; Sita garlands Rama; Parashurama storms in.
- Valmiki: the kings had come and failed EARLIER (and besieged Mithila for a year); Rama breaks the bow on a visit, at Vishvamitra's word, before
  "thousands of people"; no jaymala in the scene; Parashurama meets them on the way home after the wedding.
- Śloka: 1.72.17 (the kanyadaan), ideal for Vivah Panchami. Quote cards: Manas "सियँ जयमाल राम उर मेली" (+ maybe "भूप सहस दस") and Valmiki 1.66.17 or 1.65.19.
- Bala Kanda: consensus treats the Bala frame as a later layer; source card says BALA KANDA; avoid "Valmiki says" absolutes beyond the text read.
- Characters: Janaka (major? not in the list; ask/decide), Vishvamitra (rig exists, ask-list: major), Sita (locked), Rama, Lakshmana, Parashurama (new; likely major).
