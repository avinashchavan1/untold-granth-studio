# A82 — Who was Shanta? (research, Nov 21 2026)

Base text: Valmiki Ramayana, Critical Edition (enjoylearningsanskrit.com), whole text downloaded (see A17 notes).

## Absence / presence search (whole text)
- Raw-HTML grep "शान्ता|शान्तां|शान्तया" over all 7 kandas: the name Shanta appears ONLY in Bala 1.8, 1.9, 1.10, 1.17
  (other hits are the adjective प्रशान्त/केशान्त etc.). Nowhere is she called Dasharatha's daughter or Rama's sister.

## What Valmiki says (Bala, CE; vulgate 1.9-11)
- 1.8.1 Dasharatha has no son; 1.8.4-6 Sumantra tells Sanatkumara's old prophecy.
- 1.8.11-12 Lomapada (Romapada), king of Anga; drought because of the king's transgression.
- 1.8.15-16 Brahmins: bring Rishyashringa, son of Vibhandaka, "प्रयच्छ कन्यां शान्तां" give him the maiden Shanta.
- 1.9.28-32 rain falls as he arrives; the king gives "कन्यां … शान्तां" to him; Rishyashringa lives there with his wife Shanta.
- 1.10.3 "अङ्गराजेन सख्यं च तस्य राज्ञो भविष्यति । कन्या चास्य महाभागा शान्ता नाम भविष्यति ॥" Dasharatha will be friends with the king of
  Anga; HE (asya, the Anga king) will have a greatly fortunate daughter named Shanta.
- 1.10.5 (in the prophecy, Dasharatha to Lomapada) "अनपत्यो ऽस्मि धर्मात्मञ् …" I am childless; may Shanta's husband perform my rite.
- 1.10.15-18 Dasharatha goes to Anga, stays 7-8 days.
- 1.10.19 Dasharatha to Lomapada: "शान्ता तव सुता राजन् सह भर्त्रा विशाम्पते । मदीयं नगरं यातु कार्यं हि महदुद्यतम् ॥"
  O king, let YOUR daughter Shanta come to my city with her husband; a great task is at hand.
- 1.10.20-21 Lomapada to Rishyashringa: go with your wife. 1.10.28-29 the women of Ayodhya's inner palace welcome "the wide-eyed lady"; she stays.
- 1.17.5 after the horse-sacrifice, Rishyashringa departs with Shanta. 1.17.6 Kausalya gives birth to Rama.

## Popular version (verified source)
- Srimad Bhagavatam 9.23.7-8 (vedabase.io, Devanagari as printed):
  "सुतो धर्मरथो यस्य जज्ञे चित्ररथोऽप्रजा: । रोमपाद इति ख्यातस्तस्मै दशरथ: सखा ॥ ७ ॥
   शान्तां स्वकन्यां प्रायच्छदृष्यशृङ्ग उवाह याम् । …॥ ८ ॥"  — to Romapada, who had no children, his friend Dasharatha gave his own daughter
  Shanta, whom Rishyashringa married. (Mahabharata/other later tellings also exist; not quoted, not checked line by line.)
