## What Valmiki does say about Lakshmana and sleep (Ayodhya)
- Night 1, Tamasa bank: 2.41.13-14 — Rama and Sita asleep; Lakshmana tells Sumantra of Rama's virtues; "the sun rose while Lakshmana
  and the charioteer remained awake throughout that night".
- Night 2, Shringaverapura: 2.44.25 Lakshmana washes Rama's feet, stands leaning on a tree; 2.44.26 Guha stays awake with him;
  2.45.1 Lakshmana "awake, sincerely for his brother's sake"; 2.45.9 Lakshmana: "कथं दाशरथौ भूमौ शयाने सह सीतया । शक्या निद्रा मया लब्धुं जीवितं वा सुखानि वा ॥"
  (How can I get sleep, or life, or pleasures, while the son of Dasharatha lies on the ground with Sita?) — repeated by Guha to Bharata at 2.80.10.
- Night 3, under a tree past the Ganga: 2.47.3 Rama: "जागर्तव्यमतन्द्रिभ्यामद्य प्रभृति रात्रिषु । योगक्षेमो हि सीताया वर्तते लक्ष्मणावयोः ॥"
  (From today, we two must stay awake, untiring, at night; Sita's safety rests on us two, Lakshmana.)
- Night 4 Bharadvaja's ashram (2.48-49.1); night 5 by the Yamuna (2.49.15).
- Next morning 2.50.1: "अथ रात्र्यां व्यतीतायामवसुप्तमनन्तरम् । प्रबोधयामास शनैर्लक्ष्मणं रघुनन्दनः ॥" Rama gently woke Lakshmana, who had been sleeping beside him.
- 2.50.3: "स सुप्तः समये भ्रात्रा लक्ष्मणः प्रतिबोधितः । जहौ निद्रां च तन्द्रीं च प्रसक्तं च पथि श्रमम् ॥"
  (Lakshmana, who had been asleep, woken in time by his brother, shook off sleep, drowsiness and the fatigue of the road.)

## Popular version
- The story of Nidra Devi taking Lakshmana's sleep and Urmila sleeping 14 years is a much-loved later folk telling (widely told in
  Telugu folk songs and modern retellings). We did NOT verify a single canonical text with exact lines, so the reel names no text and
  quotes none: it says "a famous later telling", told with devotion. (Rule: only name a source we have read.)
- Related later legend (Indrajit can die only by one who has not slept 14 years) NOT claimed in this reel (not checked line by line).
