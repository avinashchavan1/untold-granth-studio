# A23: Hanuman brought the herb mountain TWICE (research notes)

Source: CE text and translation at enjoylearningsanskrit.com (book-6, chapters 61, 88, 89); Ramcharitmanas Lanka Kand at ramcharit.in.

## Trip 1: CE 6.61 (vulgate 6.74)
- 6.61.1-4: Indrajit's Brahmastra has struck down BOTH Rama and Lakshmana, and the army.
- 6.61.20-28: wounded Jambavan asks first whether Hanuman lives; tells him he alone can save them.
- 6.61.29-34: go to the Himalaya; between golden Rishabha and Kailasa is the Herb Mountain with four herbs: mṛtasaṃjīvanī, viśalyakaraṇī, sauvarṇakaraṇī, saṃdhānī.
- 6.61.50-57: Hanuman reaches the Himalaya and the blazing herb mountain.
- 6.61.58: the herbs, knowing a seeker was coming, HID from sight.
- 6.61.59-61: angry, he tells the mountain "you show no compassion for Raghava?" and tears off the whole peak.
- 6.61.63: carrying it on the sun's path he shone "like a rival sun".
- 6.61.67: the FRAGRANCE of the herbs alone heals Rama, Lakshmana and the monkey chiefs.
- 6.61.68: Hanuman then CARRIES THE MOUNTAIN BACK to the Himalaya, and returns to Rama.

## Trip 2: CE 6.88-89 (vulgate 6.101-102)
- 6.88.30-35: RAVANA (not Indrajit) hurls the Maya-made shakti; it strikes Lakshmana's chest; he falls.
- 6.89.1-8: Rama's grief ("what use is the kingdom...").
- 6.89.9-12: Sushena (a vanara chief, the army's healer) says Lakshmana is not dead.
- 6.89.13-16: Sushena sends Hanuman to the herb mountain "previously described by Jambavan" for viśalyakaraṇī and the others.
- 6.89.17-21: Hanuman doesn't know the herbs; brings the peak again: "I don't recognise them, so I brought the whole peak."
- 6.89.22-24: Sushena crushes the herb, gives it through Lakshmana's nose; Lakshmana rises, free of pain.

## The popular version (Ramcharitmanas, Lanka Kand 54-55)
- Meghnad (Indrajit) strikes Lakshmana with the shakti (54.4).
- Jambavan says the physician Sushena lives in Lanka; Hanuman brings him "house and all" (55.4); Sushena names the herb and mountain (doha 55).
- (Then Kalanemi's trap and Bharata's arrow: not in Valmiki; verify before claiming.)

## Angle
- Most people know ONE mountain trip. Valmiki has two, and the first one ends with Hanuman politely returning the mountain.
- Respectful framing for Tulsidas, as in A53.
