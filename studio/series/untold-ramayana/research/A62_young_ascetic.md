# A62 · The young ascetic Dasharatha killed had no name

Popular telling: "Shravan Kumar" carries his blind parents in a kāvaḍ (two baskets on a pole) on pilgrimage; he goes to fetch
water for them; King Dasharatha, hunting, shoots at the sound; the parents curse Dasharatha to die of grief for his son.

**Name:** none. The young man is only तापसः (2.57.27, 2.58.14), तपस्विन् (2.57.18), मुनिपुत्रः (2.58.40, 2.58.42), "your son" (2.58.15).
Searched both sargas (Sanskrit and translation) for श्रवण / any personal name: none. His parents are not named either.
**No pilgrimage, no kāvaḍ:** the parents are in their own ashram (2.57.35, 2.58.2–4); the son came to the river alone, at night, for water.

## The popular version's own words (checked)
- Ramcharitmanas, Ayodhya Kand, chaupai before doha 155 (ramcharit.in/ayodhya-kand, verbatim):
  "तापस अंध साप सुधि आई। कौसल्यहि सब कथा सुनाई।।" (He remembered the blind ascetic's curse and told Kausalya the whole story.)
  Tulsidas also gives no name.
- The name "Shravan Kumar" and the kāvaḍ pilgrimage come from later retellings; we do NOT name a specific source for them (not checked).

## Avoid / drop
- 2.57.37 (his birth: Vaishya father, Shudra mother): accurate, but not needed for the payoff and easy to sensationalise. Dropped.
- The translation of 2.57.3 adds "sixth night"; the Sanskrit only says midnight. Say "at midnight".
- 2.58.24 translation "accompanied by his wife" is a mistranslation; not used.
- Don't say what happened to the parents after the curse (not in what we read); don't call the popular story "wrong": both are told with devotion.
- Don't put a date/text on where "Shravan" first appears.
