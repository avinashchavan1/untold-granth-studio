# LF01 research: Vali's death (CE Kishkindha 4.5, 4.9-4.25)
- Base text read verse by verse via `scripts/source_verse.py 4 <sarga>`; the 159 verses we cite are in `ce_verses.json` (Devanagari, translit, English).
- English page faults: 4.18.56 says "Hanuman" but the speaker is Vali (Devanagari context); 4.19.26-28 is a mis-pasted Mandodari passage, not used;
  4.18.36 has no transliteration on the page (Devanagari only). Always quote the Devanagari, and translate it ourselves where the page is wrong.
- Popular version: Ramcharitmanas Kishkindha Kand at ramcharit.in, lines copied verbatim into `../longform.json → popular_compare`.
- Key facts the video rests on: 4.12.20/31 (Rama couldn't tell the brothers apart), 4.12.35-37 (flower creeper), 4.17.5 (Indra's garland held his life),
  4.15.14-20 (Tara's counsel), 4.16.5 (Vali: "how could he do wrong?"), 4.17.13 & 39-43 (accusation), 4.18.18-22 & 34-36 (Rama's answer),
  4.18.41 (Vali accepts), 4.22.14 ("Tara's judgement never fails"), 4.22.16 (garland to Sugriva), 4.25.9 (Rama won't enter the city).
- Absence claims: no offer of life by Rama in 4.17-4.22 (read in full); no "half strength" boon in 4.5-4.25 (Uttara unread: don't generalise).
