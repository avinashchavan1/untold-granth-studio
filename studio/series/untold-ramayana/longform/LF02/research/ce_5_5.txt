5.5.1
  स निकामं विनामेषु विचरन् कामरूपधृक् ।
  विचचार कपिर्लङ्कां लाघवेन समन्वितः ॥
  sa nikāmaṃ vināmeṣu vicaran kāmarūpadhṛk, vicacāra kapirlaṅkāṃ lāghavena samanvitaḥ
  The monkey (kapi), assuming any form he desired (kāmarūpadhṛk), moved about with great freedom, exploring the turns and bends of Laṅkā with agility.

5.5.2
  आससादाथ लक्ष्मीवान् राक्षसेन्द्रनिवेशनम् ।
  प्राकारेणार्कवर्णेन भास्वरेणाभिसंवृतम् ॥
  āsasādātha lakṣmīvān rākṣasendraniveśanam, prākāreṇārkavarṇena bhāsvareṇābhisaṃvṛtam
  Then, the fortunate (lakṣmīvān) Hanumān reached the palace of the king of rākṣasas (Rāvaṇa), which was completely encircled by a shining rampart, radiant like the sun.

5.5.3
  रक्षितं राक्षसैर्भीमैः सिंहैरिव महद्वनम् ।
  समीक्षमाणो भवनं चकाशे कपिकुञ्जरः ॥
  rakṣitaṃ rākṣasairbhīmaiḥ siṃhairiva mahadvanam, samīkṣamāṇo bhavanaṃ cakāśe kapikuñjaraḥ
  (tat) mahat vanam siṃhaiḥ iva bhīmaiḥ rākṣasaiḥ rakṣitam (āsīt).

5.5.4
  रूप्यकोपहितैश्चित्रैस्तोरणैर्हेमभूषितैः ।
  विचित्राभिश्च कक्ष्याभिर्द्वारैश्च रुचिरैर्वृतम् ॥
  rūpyakopahitaiścitraistoraṇairhemabhūṣitaiḥ, vicitrābhiśca kakṣyābhirdvāraiśca rucirairvṛtam
  (tat) rūpyakopahitaiḥ hemabhūṣitaiḥ citraiḥ toraṇaiḥ,

5.5.5
  गजास्थितैर्महामात्रैः शूरैश्च विगतश्रमैः ।
  उपस्थितमसंहार्यैर्हयैः स्यन्दनयायिभिः ॥
  gajāsthitairmahāmātraiḥ śūraiśca vigataśramaiḥ, upasthitamasaṃhāryairhayaiḥ syandanayāyibhiḥ
  The place was attended by high officials mounted on elephants, by valiant and tireless heroes, by irresistible horses, and by charioteers.

5.5.6
  सिंहव्याघ्रतनुत्राणैर्दान्तकाञ्चनराजतैः ।
  घोषवद्भिर्विचित्रैश्च सदा विचरितं रथैः ॥
  siṃhavyāghratanutrāṇairdāntakāñcanarājataiḥ, ghoṣavadbhirvicitraiśca sadā vicaritaṃ rathaiḥ
  And splendid, resounding chariots, adorned with lion and tiger motifs on their armor, and embellished with ivory, gold, and silver, always moved about.

5.5.7
  बहुरत्नसमाकीर्णं परार्ध्यासनभाजनम् ।
  महारथसमावासं महारथमहासनम् ॥
  bahuratnasamākīrṇaṃ parārdhyāsanabhājanam, mahārathasamāvāsaṃ mahārathamahāsanam
  It was strewn with many jewels, equipped with most precious seats, a dwelling for great warriors (mahāratha), and a grand throne for great warriors (mahāratha).

5.5.8
  दृश्यैश्च परमोदारैस्तैस्तैश्च मृगपक्षिभिः ।
  विविधैर्बहुसाहस्रैः परिपूर्णं समन्ततः ॥
  dṛśyaiśca paramodāraistaistaiśca mṛgapakṣibhiḥ, vividhairbahusāhasraiḥ paripūrṇaṃ samantataḥ
  And it was completely filled on all sides by visible, most splendid, and various kinds of animals and birds, diverse and numbering in many thousands.

5.5.9
  विनीतैरन्तपालैश्च रक्षोभिश्च सुरक्षितम् ।
  मुख्याभिश्च वरस्त्रीभिः परिपूर्णं समन्ततः ॥
  vinītairantapālaiśca rakṣobhiśca surakṣitam, mukhyābhiśca varastrībhiḥ paripūrṇaṃ samantataḥ
  That place (the city or palace) is well-protected by disciplined border guards and rākṣasas, and completely filled on all sides by chief and excellent women.

5.5.10
  मुदितप्रमदा रत्नं राक्षसेन्द्रनिवेशनम् ।
  वराभरणनिर्ह्रादैः समुद्रस्वननिःस्वनम् ॥
  muditapramadā ratnaṃ rākṣasendraniveśanam, varābharaṇanirhrādaiḥ samudrasvananiḥsvanam
  The palace of the lord of rākṣasas, which is a treasure with joyous women, and whose sounds from excellent ornaments resemble the roar of the ocean.

5.5.11
  तद् राजगुणसंपन्नं मुख्यैश्च वरचन्दनैः ।
  भेरीमृदङ्गाभिरुतं शङ्खघोषविनादितम् ॥
  tad rājaguṇasaṃpannaṃ mukhyaiśca varacandanaiḥ, bherīmṛdaṅgābhirutaṃ śaṅkhaghoṣavināditam
  That (palace), endowed with royal attributes and adorned with choice sandal pastes, resounds with the sounds of kettledrums and mṛdaṅgas, and is made vibrant by the noise of conch shells.

5.5.12
  नित्यार्चितं पर्वहुतं पूजितं राक्षसैः सदा ।
  समुद्रमिव गम्भीरं समुद्रमिव निःस्वनम् ॥
  nityārcitaṃ parvahutaṃ pūjitaṃ rākṣasaiḥ sadā, samudramiva gambhīraṃ samudramiva niḥsvanam
  Always revered and receiving ritual offerings at auspicious times, it is continually worshipped by rākṣasas. It is profound like the ocean, and its sound is like the ocean's roar.

5.5.13
  महात्मानो महद्वेश्म महारत्नपरिच्छदम् ।
  महाजनसमाकीर्णं ददर्श स महाकपिः ॥
  mahātmāno mahadveśma mahāratnaparicchadam, mahājanasamākīrṇaṃ dadarśa sa mahākapiḥ
  The great monkey (mahākapi) saw noble individuals (mahātmānaḥ), a magnificent palace, richly adorned with great jewels, and bustling with many important people.

5.5.14
  विराजमानं वपुषा गजाश्वरथसंकुलम् ।
  लङ्काभरणमित्येव सो ऽमन्यत महाकपिः ॥
  virājamānaṃ vapuṣā gajāśvarathasaṃkulam, laṅkābharaṇamityeva so'manyata mahākapiḥ
  Shining brilliantly by its very form and teeming with elephants, horses, and chariots, the great monkey (mahākapi) indeed regarded it as the very ornament of Laṅkā.

5.5.15
  गृहाद्गृहं राक्षसानामुद्यानानि च वानरः ।
  वीक्षमाणो ह्यसंत्रस्तः प्रासादांश्च चचार सः ॥
  gṛhādgṛhaṃ rākṣasānāmudyānāni ca vānaraḥ, vīkṣamāṇo hyasaṃtrastaḥ prāsādāṃśca cacāra saḥ
  The monkey (vānara), indeed fearless, moved from one house of the rākṣasas to another, observing their gardens and palaces.

5.5.16
  अवप्लुत्य महावेगः प्रहस्तस्य निवेशनम् ।
  ततो ऽन्यत् पुप्लुवे वेश्म महापार्श्वस्य वीर्यवान् ॥
  avaplutya mahāvegaḥ prahastasya niveśanam, tato'nyat pupluve veśma mahāpārśvasya vīryavān
  Having alighted with great speed (mahāvega) upon Prahastha's dwelling, the powerful one (vīryavān) then sprang to another residence, that of Mahāpārśva.

5.5.17
  अथ मेघप्रतीकाशं कुम्भकर्णनिवेशनम् ।
  विभीषणस्य च तथा पुप्लुवे स महाकपिः ॥
  atha meghapratīkāśaṃ kumbhakarṇaniveśanam, vibhīṣaṇasya ca tathā pupluve sa mahākapiḥ
  Then, that great monkey (Hanuman) flew over Kumbhakarna's dwelling, which was like a cloud, and similarly over Vibhishana's residence.

5.5.18
  विद्युज्जिह्वस्य भवनं विद्युन्मालेस्तथैव च ।
  वज्रदंष्ट्रस्य च तथा पुप्लुवे स महाकपिः ॥
  
  That great monkey (Hanuman) also flew over the residences of Mahodara, and similarly over Virupaksha's, and indeed over Vidyutjihva's dwelling; likewise over Vidyunmali's, and similarly over Vajradamṣṭra's.

5.5.19
  शुकस्य च महावेगः सारणस्य च धीमतः ।
  तथा चेन्द्रजितो वेश्म जगाम हरियूथपः ॥
  śukasya ca mahāvegaḥ sāraṇasya ca dhīmataḥ, tathā cendrajito veśma jagāma hariyūthapaḥ
  And that immensely swift chief of the monkeys (Hanuman) went to the dwelling of Shuka, and of the intelligent Sarana, and similarly to Indrajit's.

5.5.20
  जम्बुमालेः सुमालेश्च जगाम हरियूथपः ।
  रश्मिकेतोश्च भवनं सूर्यशत्रोस्तथैव च ॥
  jambumāleḥ sumāleśca jagāma hariyūthapaḥ, raśmiketośca bhavanaṃ sūryaśatrostathaiva ca
  The chief of the monkeys (Hanuman) went to the residences of Jambumali and Sumali; and similarly, to the dwelling of Rashmiketu and Suryashatru.

5.5.21
  धूम्राक्षस्य च संपातेर्भवनं मारुतात्मजः ।
  विद्युद्रूपस्य भीमस्य घनस्य विघनस्य च ॥
  dhūmrākṣasya ca saṃpāterbhavanaṃ mārutātmajaḥ, vidyudrūpasya bhīmasya ghanasya vighanasya ca
  Hanūman, the son of Vāyu, was surveying the dwelling of Dhūmrākṣa and Saṃpāti, and of Vidyudrūpa, Bhīma, Ghana, and Vighana.

5.5.22
  शुकनाभस्य वक्रस्य शठस्य विकटस्य च ।
  ह्रस्वकर्णस्य दंष्ट्रस्य रोमशस्य च रक्षसः ॥
  śukanābhasya vakrasya śaṭhasya vikaṭasya ca, hrasvakarṇasya daṃṣṭrasya romaśasya ca rakṣasaḥ
  He was also surveying the dwellings of Śukanābha, Vakra, Śaṭha, and Vikaṭa, and of the rākṣasas Hrasvakarṇa, Daṃṣṭra, and Romaśa.

5.5.23
  युद्धोन्मत्तस्य मत्तस्य ध्वजग्रीवस्य नादिनः ।
  विद्युज्जिह्वेन्द्रजिह्वानां तथा हस्तिमुखस्य च ॥
  yuddhonmattasya mattasya dhvajagrīvasya nādinaḥ, vidyujjihvendrajihvānāṃ tathā hastimukhasya ca
  He also surveyed those of Yuddhōnmattā, Matta, Dhvajagrīva, and Nādin; and of Vidyujjihva and Indrajihva, as well as Hastimukha.

5.5.24
  करालस्य पिशाचस्य शोणिताक्षस्य चैव हि ।
  क्रममाणः क्रमेणैव हनूमान्मारुतात्मजः ॥
  karālasya piśācasya śoṇitākṣasya caiva hi, kramamāṇaḥ krameṇaiva hanūmānmārutātmajaḥ
  And indeed, [he surveyed the dwellings] of Karāla, Piśāca, and Śoṇitākṣa. Hanūman, the son of Vāyu, was thus proceeding gradually.

5.5.25
  तेषु तेषु महार्हेषु भवनेषु महायशाः ।
  तेषामृद्धिमतामृद्धिं ददर्श स महाकपिः ॥
  teṣu teṣu mahārheṣu bhavaneṣu mahāyaśāḥ, teṣāmṛddhimatāmṛddhiṃ dadarśa sa mahākapiḥ
  The greatly renowned great monkey (Hanuman) saw the prosperity of those opulent inhabitants within one splendid mansion after another.

5.5.26
  सर्वेषां समतिक्रम्य भवनानि समन्ततः ।
  आससादाथ लक्ष्मीवान् राक्षसेन्द्रनिवेशनम् ॥
  sarveṣāṃ samatikramya bhavanāni samantataḥ, āsasādātha lakṣmīvān rākṣasendraniveśanam
  Then, the glorious (Hanuman), having surpassed all the mansions on every side, reached the dwelling of the king of the Rākṣasas.

5.5.27
  विचरन् हरिशार्दूलो राक्षसीर्विकृतेक्षणाः ।
  शूलमुद्गरहस्ताश्च शक्तो तोमरधारिणीः ॥
  
  As he wandered, the monkey-tiger (Hanuman), best among monkeys, saw female Rākṣasas lying near Rāvaṇa. They had distorted eyes, held spears and maces in their hands, and bore śakti weapons and javelins.

5.5.29
  रक्ताञ् श्वेतान् सितांश्चैव हरींश्चैव महाजवान् ।
  कुलीनान् रूपसंपन्नान् गजान्परगजारुजान् ॥
  raktāñ śvetān sitāṃścaiva harīṃścaiva mahājavān, kulīnān rūpasaṃpannān gajānparagajārujān
  He saw elephants that were red, white, pale, and tawny, possessing great swiftness, of noble lineage, endowed with beauty, and capable of overpowering enemy elephants.

5.5.30
  निष्ठितान् गजशिखायामैरावतसमान्युधि ।
  निहन्तॄन्परसैन्यानां गृहे तस्मिन्ददर्श सः ॥
  niṣṭhitān gajaśikhāyāmairāvatasamānyudhi, nihantṝnparasainyānāṃ gṛhe tasmindadarśa saḥ
  In that palace, he saw those elephants, skilled in elephant training, resembling Airāvata in battle, and destroyers of enemy armies.

5.5.31
  क्षरतश्च यथा मेघान् स्रवतश्च यथा गिरीन् ।
  मेघस्तनितनिर्घोषान्दुर्धर्षान् समरे परैः ॥
  kṣarataśca yathā meghān sravataśca yathā girīn, meghastanitanirghoṣāndurdharṣān samare paraiḥ
  They were like clouds dripping moisture and like mountains flowing with streams, possessing a roar like the thunder of clouds, and were irresistible to enemies in battle.

5.5.32
  सहस्रं वाहिनीस्तत्र जाम्बूनदपरिष्कृताः ।
  हेमजालैरविच्छिन्नास्तरुणादित्यसंनिभाः ॥
  sahasraṃ vāhinīstatra jāmbūnadapariṣkṛtāḥ, hemajālairavicchinnāstaruṇādityasaṃnibhāḥ
  There, a thousand divisions of forces, adorned with Jāmbūnada gold, continuously covered with golden networks, shone like the rising sun.

5.5.33
  ददर्श राक्षसेन्द्रस्य रावणस्य निवेशने ।
  शिबिका विविधाकाराः स कपिर्मारुतात्मजः ॥
  dadarśa rākṣasendrasya rāvaṇasya niveśane, śibikā vividhākārāḥ sa kapirmārutātmajaḥ
  That monkey, the son of Maruta (Vāyu), saw palanquins of various forms in the abode of Rāvaṇa, the king of rākṣasas.

5.5.34
  लतागृहाणि चित्राणि चित्रशालागृहाणि च ।
  क्रीडागृहाणि चान्यानि दारुपर्वतकानपि ॥
  latāgṛhāṇi citrāṇi citraśālāgṛhāṇi ca, krīḍāgṛhāṇi cānyāni dāruparvatakānapi
  He also saw wonderful creeper-houses, picture-galleries, other playhouses, and even artificial wooden mountains.

5.5.35
  कामस्य गृहकं रम्यं दिवागृहकमेव च ।
  ददर्श राक्षसेन्द्रस्य रावणस्य निवेशने ॥
  kāmasya gṛhakaṃ ramyaṃ divāgṛhakameva ca, dadarśa rākṣasendrasya rāvaṇasya niveśane
  (saḥ) rākṣasendrasya rāvaṇasya niveśane ramyam

5.5.36
  स मन्दरतलप्रख्यं मयूरस्थानसंकुलम् ।
  ध्वजयष्टिभिराकीर्णं ददर्श भवनोत्तमम् ॥
  sa mandaratalaprakhyaṃ mayūrasthānasaṃkulam, dhvajayaṣṭibhirākīrṇaṃ dadarśa bhavanottamam
  He saw the most excellent mansion, resembling the base of Mount Mandara, crowded with peacock perches, and covered with flagstaffs.

5.5.37
  अनन्तरत्ननिचयं निधिजालं समन्ततः ।
  धीरनिष्ठितकर्मान्तं गृहं भूतपतेरिव ॥
  anantaratnanicayaṃ nidhijālaṃ samantataḥ, dhīraniṣṭhitakarmāntaṃ gṛhaṃ bhūtapateriva
  The abode, like that of the lord of beings, was filled with countless heaps of jewels and a multitude of treasures all around, its extensive works meticulously completed by the steadfast.

5.5.38
  अर्चिर्भिश्चापि रत्नानां तेजसा रावणस्य च ।
  विरराजाथ तद्वेश्म रश्मिमानिव रश्मिभिः ॥
  arcirbhiścāpi ratnānāṃ tejasā rāvaṇasya ca, virarājātha tadveśma raśmimāniva raśmibhiḥ
  And that abode, then, shone brilliantly with the luster of jewels and also with the radiance of Rāvaṇa, just as the sun shines with its own rays.

5.5.39
  जाम्बूनदमयान्येव शयनान्यासनानि च ।
  भाजनानि च शुभ्राणि ददर्श हरियूथपः ॥
  jāmbūnadamayānyeva śayanānyāsanāni ca, bhājanāni ca śubhrāṇi dadarśa hariyūthapaḥ
  The leader of the monkeys (Hanuman) saw beds and seats, all indeed made of pure gold, and gleaming clean vessels.

5.5.40
  मध्वासवकृतक्लेदं मणिभाजनसंकुलम् ।
  मनोरममसंबाधं कुबेरभवनं यथा ॥
  madhvāsavakṛtakledaṃ maṇibhājanasaṃkulam, manoramamasaṃbādhaṃ kuberabhavanaṃ yathā
  It was charming, spacious, moistened by honey-wine, and crowded with jeweled vessels, just like the abode of Kubera.

5.5.41
  नूपुराणां च घोषेण काञ्चीनां निनदेन च ।
  मृदङ्गतलघोषैश्च घोषवद्भिर्विनादितम् ॥
  nūpurāṇāṃ ca ghoṣeṇa kāñcīnāṃ ninadena ca, mṛdaṅgatalaghoṣaiśca ghoṣavadbhirvināditam
  The mansion was made to resound with the clamor of anklets, the tinkling of waist-bells, and the beats of drums.

5.5.42
  प्रासादसंघातयुतं स्त्रीरत्नशतसंकुलम् ।
  सुव्यूढकक्ष्यं हनुमान्प्रविवेश महागृहम् ॥
  prāsādasaṃghātayutaṃ strīratnaśatasaṃkulam, suvyūḍhakakṣyaṃ hanumānpraviveśa mahāgṛham
  Hanumān entered the magnificent mansion, adorned with numerous palaces, bustling with hundreds of excellent women, and featuring well-arranged chambers.
