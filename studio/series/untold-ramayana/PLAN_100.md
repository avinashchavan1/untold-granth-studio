# Untold Ramayana: plan for the next 100 reels

**Order.** Growth first. The first 20 reels are the strongest myth-busters and "wait, what?" reveals; that is what gets shared. After that, myth-busters stay roughly 1 in 3 so the page keeps its identity.

**References.** Each row gives an *approximate* location (Kanda, plus the vulgate/Gita Press sarga in brackets).

- The Critical Edition chapter number is usually a few lower. In Aranya Kanda the vulgate number is about the CE number + 2; in the other Kandas the gap varies.
- **No row is verified yet.** Step 2 of the skill (read the verses, fill `claims[]`) happens before scripting.
- **⚠ marks a row with a known risk.** Either my memory of the text is uncertain, or the episode sits in a later layer (Bala/Uttara), or it is sensitive. These need extra care or may be dropped.

**Type key:** **M** = myth-buster (popular belief vs. Valmiki) · **R** = little-known reveal · **D** = dialogue people never heard.

**New-art cost:** ● = reuses existing characters and locations · ●● = one new character or location · ●●● = several new ones.

## Corrections to the original brief (please read)

1. **"Ravana never touched Sita" is not true in Valmiki.** He seizes her by the hair and thigh and carries her off (Aranya, abduction sargas).
   - The real untold story is *why he never forced himself on her*: curses that would kill him (Nalakubara's curse in the Uttara Kanda; Ravana himself mentions a curse in the Yuddha Kanda).
   - It is reframed as reel #22.
2. **Rama's colour.** Valmiki repeatedly calls Rama *śyāma* (dark, like a rain cloud). The brief asked for "fair, NOT blue/dark".
   - The current rig follows the brief. A Valmiki-accurate page should consider a dusky Rama; this is your call.
   - Either way, decide once and lock it.

## Production rhythm and budget

- **One reel at a time.** Verify → script → art → voices → render → review. About 3–5 hours of my working time per reel; new characters and locations cost the most.
- **ElevenLabs narrator:** about 0.8–1.3k characters per reel including retakes.
  - The Starter plan (40k/month) covers about 30 reels a month. 100 reels need about 110–130k characters (≈ 3–4 months of Starter, or one Creator month).
- **Character voices** (Chatterbox, local): free, but CPU-slow (about 1–2 minutes per take, 4 takes per line).
- **Reuse order.** Reels marked ● reuse existing art, so cluster them. Build each new character once (Hanuman, Sugriva, Ravana in full, Vibhishana, Bharata, Kaikeyi, Dasharatha), lock it, and it becomes cheap afterwards.
